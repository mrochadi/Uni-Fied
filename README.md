# Uni-Fied
JMMD SYNCS Hack 2026 project.

This project unifies the foundational blocks that make up our world - specifically in a university-based context.
Uni-fied, a university social media website, aims to connect people within the University of Sydney to the USyd community, 
allowing students to access information, opportunities and connections in a more accessible and centralised way. 
This service provides many features such as:
- Posts / Discussion boards
- Timetable Matcher
- Map
- Society / University Events
- People based features (incl. chat, group chat and suggested connections)

The process to start using this service is simple:
- Login using your student Unikey. Your timetable, degree (units) and SID will be connected automatically to your profile.
- Connect your USU for society-based information (optional).
- Add your preferences and customise your profile (users can choose which information is public and private).

Future goals for Uni-Fied will be to implement itself as a user-friendly app for enhanced accessability.
